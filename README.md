# UPDATES

## Abstract:
### The account "istekdev" on Github will no longer be operational for the foreseeable future. Instead, all remaining and future projects will take place on Codeberg.

---

## Why?
### Over the past year as "istekdev," I've been slowly adopting the philosophy of individualism as a result of knowing that everything I do on the internet is being tracked, archived, sold, and used for AI training. Additionally, past scandals (e.g. Titanpointe); the increase in cloud dependency; and the rise of IAM (Identity and Access Management) in previously-free applications contributed to my sense of paranoia. Consequently, this has reshaped my desire towards privacy-focused FOSS. In conclusion, my decision to migrate from Github to Codeberg is a direct result of my growing individualism.
